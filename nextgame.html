Pokémon™
Pokopia

In this cozy life-sim, you play as a Ditto transformed into a human working alongside Professor Tangrowth to build a thriving town for Pokémon. By befriending Pokémon and learning their moves, you can transform the environment, using abilities like Leafage to grow grass or Surf to cross water. Experience a relaxing journey with a real-time day/night cycle and dynamic weather as you gather materials, craft furniture, cultivate crops, and design custom habitats for your growing community.

So far it's looking like Animal Crossing but make it Pokémon, I'm excited!
